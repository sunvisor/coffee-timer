A few things to know.

Do I need to install anything?

You can start right in your browser. For a home screen shortcut, use your Android browser's install option, or Safari's Add to Home Screen on iPhone. After the first successful load and service worker setup, the app also works offline.

Where do my recipes and records go?

They stay in your browser. No account or server upload is needed, and devices do not sync automatically. Export recipes as JSON in Settings. Brew records keep up to 100 entries and approximately 1 MB, removing older records when needed. They are not included in recipe backups.

Can I close the screen while brewing?

Keep the screen open while brewing. Supported browsers prevent automatic screen-off from recipe preparation onwards, but power-saving settings may prevent this. Tones use your device's media volume. Background execution and playback during sleep are not guaranteed.
